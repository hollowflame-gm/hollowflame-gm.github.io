Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep — Support

Thanks for playing Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep. If something is not working, or you just have a question, the answers below cover the most common cases. If none of them helps, email us and we will get back to you.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Create a character, choose one of the nine classes and descend into Cinderdeep. Fight through the floors, collect gear and sigils, and rest at Bonfires to spend points, equip abilities, repair and travel. Every fifth floor ends with a boss. The Help screen in the menu explains the controls.

I bought the Supporter Pack, but it is locked on my new device

Open the Supporter Pack screen from the main menu and tap Restore purchase. Sign in with the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, not to a device, and restoring is free.

Restore purchase says there is nothing to restore

This almost always means the device is signed in to a different Apple Account than the one that made the purchase. Check Settings > [your name] > Media & Purchases on your device, then try again.

My achievements do not appear in Game Center

Make sure you are signed in to Game Center (Settings > Game Center on iPhone and iPad, System Settings > Game Center on Mac). Achievements unlocked while you were offline or signed out are sent the next time you start the Game while signed in.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Yes. Characters and progress are stored on the device only, so deleting the app removes them. The Supporter Pack is not lost; restore it as described above.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The Game is fully playable offline. A connection is only needed to buy or restore the Supporter Pack and to send achievements to Game Center.

Are there ads?

No. The Game contains no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

The music or sound effects will not play

Check the mute switch and the volume of your device, and the music and sound sliders in the Game's Settings screen. If the device is connected to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker, audio is sent there instead.

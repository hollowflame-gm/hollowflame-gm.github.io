Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Hollow Flame Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep (the "Game"), available on the Apple App Store for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Game.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information.

Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep is designed with your privacy in mind. The Game:

  • Does not require account creation or login
  • Does not collect personal data such as name, email, or phone number
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not contain advertisements
  • Does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies

Local Data Storage

The Game stores your characters, progress, settings and unlocked achievements locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server of ours
  • Is not accessible by us or any third party
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the Game

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the Game reports the achievements you unlock to Game Center so they appear in your Game Center profile. This is handled entirely by Apple under your Apple Account; we do not receive your Game Center identity or any other data from it. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device settings, and the Game remains fully playable.

In-App Purchases

The Game offers one optional in-app purchase, the Supporter Pack. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

To let you restore a purchase you have already paid for, the Game asks the App Store which items your Apple Account owns. This check happens between your device and Apple; the answer is stored only on your device, and we never see it.

Children's Privacy

Our Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no risk of children's data being collected or stored.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses Apple's Game Center and In-App Purchase services only. Apple's privacy policy applies to those services:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

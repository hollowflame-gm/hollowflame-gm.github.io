Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep — Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Hollow Flame Games develops Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep (the “Game”). This policy covers the Android application with package name com.hollowflame.ascensioncinderdeep.

Gameplay and local storage

The Game does not require a separate game account or login. Characters, progress, settings and a cache of purchase ownership are stored on your device. We do not operate a gameplay or cloud-save server and the Game does not upload your saved progress to us. Android may back up or restore application data according to your device and Google account settings.

The Game has no advertisements, advertising identifier integration or developer-operated analytics service. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or location. We do not sell personal information or use gameplay data for targeted advertising.

Google Play purchases

The optional Supporter Pack uses Google Play Billing. Google processes payments. The Game receives product identifiers, purchase status and purchase tokens to acknowledge purchases and unlock or restore the pack. Purchase checks occur when the store connection starts and when you purchase or restore. These checks are handled on the device and through Google Play, without a developer-operated purchase server.

We do not receive your card number or payment credentials. Google may provide us with transaction records and sales reports for purchase administration and support. Google processes its payment and account information under its own Privacy Policy.

Billing diagnostics

The included Google Play Billing library sends technical diagnostic events to Google through Google DataTransport. These events concern billing calls, connection and purchase-flow results and errors, together with application and device configuration and billing-session identifiers. Device information can include the Android version, device model and manufacturer, language and region settings, network type and event time. Billing-session identifiers are not advertising identifiers.

This diagnostic processing supports the operation and analysis of Google's billing service. It can occur when the Game connects to billing even if you do not make a purchase. The Game does not provide a switch to disable the library's diagnostics. Diagnostic transfers use HTTPS and may be queued locally before transmission. We do not receive these individual diagnostic records or use them to track gameplay. Google's own privacy and retention rules apply.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use these to respond to your request and address problems. Please do not send passwords, payment credentials or information you do not want included in your support request.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Clear the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove local game data. This removes local progress and does not refund purchases or erase Google's records. Device backups may be restored according to your Android settings. Google Play can restore ownership of a purchased Supporter Pack.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. You may email us to request access, correction or deletion of support information you have provided. We cannot promise deletion of billing diagnostics or purchase records held by Google; Google's account controls, privacy policy and retention obligations govern those records. There is no separate Game account to delete.

Audience

The Game is intended for players aged 16 and over. It is not designed for children under 16. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children; contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information through support.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its date when these practices change. For privacy or support questions, contact Hollow Flame Games at hollowflame.gm@gmail.com.

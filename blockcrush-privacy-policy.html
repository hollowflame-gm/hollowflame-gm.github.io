Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Hollow Flame Games ("we", "our", or "us") operates Block Crush Ultra (the "Game"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our Game.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal information.

Block Crush Ultra is designed with your privacy in mind. The Game:

  • Does not require account creation or login
  • Does not collect personal data such as name, email, or phone number
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not contain advertisements

Local Data Storage

The Game stores your progress (save files, settings, high scores, and preferences) locally on your device only. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not accessible by us or any third party
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the Game or clearing app data

Device Sensors

The Game may use your device's motion sensors (gyroscope/accelerometer) solely to create a parallax visual effect in the background. Sensor data is processed in real time on your device only and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

In-App Purchases

The Game offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive, store, or have access to your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Our Game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no risk of children's data being collected or stored.

Third-Party Services

The Game uses Google Play Services for in-app purchases. Google's privacy policy applies to their services: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

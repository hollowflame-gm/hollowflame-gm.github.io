Ascension Endless: Cinderdeep — Android Support

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Contact Hollow Flame Games at hollowflame.gm@gmail.com. Include your device model, Android version, Game version and what happened. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Playing the Game

Create a character, choose a class and descend through Cinderdeep. The in-game Help screen explains the controls. The main game works offline and does not require a game account. Google Play purchases and restoration require a connection.

Supporter Pack and restoration

Open Supporter Pack from the title screen and choose Restore purchase. Use the same Google account in Google Play that made the purchase. Availability of purchases depends on the release and Google Play account. Purchases made through Apple are separate and do not transfer to Google Play.

Progress and deletion

Progress is saved on the device. Clearing app storage or uninstalling can remove progress. The Game does not provide its own cloud saves. Android may restore device backups depending on system settings. Purchase ownership can be restored through Google Play.

Refunds

Google Play handles payment and refund requests for Google Play purchases. See Google Play's refund instructions. This does not limit any statutory rights you may have.

Sound and controls

Check device volume and the Game's sound and music settings. Touch controls are available on Android. If the problem continues, email us with a description and, if useful, a screenshot.
